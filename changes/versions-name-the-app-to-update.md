- **When the phone and the watch speak different versions, Sukrara says which to update**: a watch app that can't
  read the phone's payload keeps showing the current glucose and names the app to update (Sukrara Aura under the
  value, the watch app with *Open on phone* to that update, the data field in its label), and the phone's Garmin page
  names it too, with one button to its update (the Connect IQ listing, or Sukrara's own). Entries from the watch wait until both match.
