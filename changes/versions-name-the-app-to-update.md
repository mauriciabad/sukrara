- **When the phone and the watch speak different versions, Sukrara says which to update**: a watch app that can't
  read the phone's payload keeps showing the current glucose, and the phone's Garmin page names the app to update with
  one button to its update (the Connect IQ listing, or Sukrara's own). Entries from the watch wait until both match.
