Sukrara Aura puts your glucose on your wrist: the value and its trend, where it sits on the rail, what Sukrara asks of
you, and how old the reading is. This is the face itself, drawn by the same code its pictures come from, with made-up
readings.

<FacePlayground />

**Start from** shows any of the states the face is checked against. **Play**, under the face, runs a scenario a few
hours in seconds, at the speed you pick: new readings arrive, the bead glides, the reading ages, waits count down. Drag
the timeline to go back and forth, and change the dial or the settings while it plays.
