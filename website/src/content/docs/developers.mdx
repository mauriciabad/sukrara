Sukrara is built in the open. Every feature starts as an investigation: a standalone proof of concept with a written
report of what was tried, what worked and what was measured. Then it's built anew in the apps, which are tested against
the same shared contracts: alert rules and timeline scenarios, the glucose card's layout, the settings schema and the
sharing protocol. These pages are the repository's own `docs/` folder, published as they are.
