## Get it

<Download app="mac" />

<Steps>

1. Click **Download for Mac** above, open the downloaded file and drag **Sukrara** onto **Applications**. Or install
   it with the Homebrew command.
2. Open Sukrara. It's signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app, and it lives in the menu bar (no
   Dock icon).
3. Follow someone: paste the `https://sukrara.mauri.app/join#…` link their phone shares, scan its QR code with the
   Mac's camera, or pair over Bluetooth nearby. They allow this Mac on their phone.

</Steps>

Sukrara updates itself: once a day it looks for a new version, signed by Sukrara and checked before it installs, and
offers it in its panel; **Install and Relaunch** is one click. Homebrew leaves updates to the app.

## What it does

- **Menu bar:** the value and trend arrow, in red or orange when out of range and struck through when the reading is
  old, and how the readings come: nearby over Bluetooth, over the internet, or offline.
- **The panel:** the same card and chart as the phone, mute and unmute, and every window one click away.
- **Alerts:** the same alerts and sounds as the phone, with the wearer's thresholds or this Mac's own.
- **Treatments:** add insulin, carbs and notes, now or earlier; they reach the wearer's log if they allow it.
- **Glucose report:** time in ranges, average glucose, GMI and variability over 1 to 30 days, with a CSV export.
- **Widgets** on the desktop and in Notification Center.
- **Juggluco on your home network:** read glucose straight from Juggluco instead of following someone.

## Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 15 or newer.
- Someone's phone with [Sukrara for Android](/android/) to follow (yours or theirs), or Juggluco on the same network.

## Versions

The **GitHub version** (the download above and Homebrew) updates itself as above. The **Mac App Store version** is the
same app, sandboxed and updated by the App Store, so without its own updater. **About Sukrara** says which one you
have.

## Questions

<details>
<summary>Do I need an iPhone, an account or a cloud service?</summary>

No. Sukrara for Mac follows a phone running Sukrara for Android directly: over Bluetooth when you're near each other,
over public Nostr relays from anywhere, end-to-end encrypted. Nothing to sign up for.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it work on an Intel Mac?</summary>

No, it needs Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 15 or newer.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Will the alerts sound during a Focus?</summary>

A Focus (Do Not Disturb, Sleep…) holds Sukrara's alerts back like any app's, unless you let Sukrara through: **Settings
› Alerts › Let Sukrara Through**. The panel warns you when notifications, banners or sounds are off for Sukrara.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can it read my sensor?</summary>

Not by itself: the sensor is read by the phone it's paired with. The Mac follows that phone, or reads Juggluco on your
home network.

</details>

<Aside type="caution" title="A preview, not a medical device">
Sukrara is a preview (0.x), not cleared or approved by any regulator. Don't make treatment decisions from it.
</Aside>
