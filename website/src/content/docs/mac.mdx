Your glucose in the menu bar, about a second behind the phone, with the same alarms. It follows a phone with
[Sukrara for Android](/android/): yours, or the person you look after.

<Gallery shots={[['macPanel', 300], ['macReport', 380]]} />

## Get it

<Download app="mac" />

<Steps>

1. Open the downloaded file and drag **Sukrara** onto **Applications**.
2. Open Sukrara. It lives in the menu bar and updates itself.
3. Follow someone: paste their invite link, scan their QR code, or pair over Bluetooth nearby.

</Steps>

## What it does

- The value and trend in the menu bar; out of range in colour, old readings struck through.
- The same card, alarms and sounds as the phone.
- Add insulin, carbs and notes to the wearer's log, if they allow it.
- A glucose report over 1 to 30 days, with a CSV export.
- Widgets on the desktop and in Notification Center.
- Or read Juggluco on your home network instead.

<Gallery shots={[['macTreatment', 340], ['macWidget', 300]]} />

## Requirements

- Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 15 or newer.
- A phone with [Sukrara for Android](/android/) to follow, or Juggluco on the same network.

## Versions

The GitHub version (the download and Homebrew) updates itself. The Mac App Store version is the same app, updated by
the App Store. **About Sukrara** says which one you have.

## Questions

<details>
<summary>Do I need an iPhone, an account or a cloud service?</summary>

No. It follows a phone with Sukrara for Android directly: over Bluetooth nearby, or over Nostr relays from anywhere,
end-to-end encrypted.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it work on an Intel Mac?</summary>

No, it needs Apple silicon (M1 or newer).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do alerts ring during a Focus?</summary>

Only if you let Sukrara through: **Settings › Alerts › Let Sukrara Through**. The panel tells you when notifications or
sounds are off.

</details>
