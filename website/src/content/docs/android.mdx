## Get it

<Download app="android" />

<Steps>

1. On your phone, tap **Download for Android**.
2. Open the file and tap **Install**. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser first.
3. Open Sukrara. Setup asks only what alerts need; the rest can wait.

</Steps>

## What it does

### If you wear the sensor

- Reads a FreeStyle Libre 3 directly; other sensors directly or through Juggluco.
- Your glucose in widgets, the notification, the status bar and the lock screen.
- Alerts with your thresholds and [sounds](/sounds/), quiet once you've acted.
- Insulin, carbs and notes in seconds. A NovoPen Echo® Plus logs its own doses.
- Uploads to LibreView, Tidepool, Nightscout and Health Connect, if you want.
- A report for your doctor, and a CSV export.

### If you follow someone

- Open their invite link or scan their QR code.
- Their glucose, widgets and alerts, with your own alert settings if you want.
- You see when their phone stops sending.

## Requirements

- Android 16 or newer, 64-bit.
- To read a sensor: NFC and Bluetooth. Starting a Libre 3 needs a LibreView account.
- Lock screen card and status bar text: root with LSPosed.

## Versions

The GitHub and the Google Play version are the same app, signed with the same key: either one installs over the other
and keeps your data. F-Droid and Obtainium install the GitHub version. **Settings › About** shows which one you have.

| | GitHub version | Google Play version |
|---|---|---|
| Updates | Sukrara updates itself once you turn it on, or F-Droid or Obtainium does | Google Play |
| Leaves out | Nothing | Only Sukrara's own updater |

## Questions

<details>
<summary>Will the alerts wake me, even in Do Not Disturb?</summary>

Very low and extremely high ring like an alarm clock. To ring through Do Not Disturb, choose **Let Sukrara through** in
**Settings › Alert reliability**; that page also checks everything else alerts need and sends a test alert.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why does Android warn me about the download?</summary>

It warns about every app from outside a store. Each release is built from the public source code and signed with
Sukrara's key; **Check it's the real Sukrara**, under the download button, shows how to verify it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I keep using Abbott's Libre app?</summary>

A Libre 3 talks to one app at a time. Sukrara starts sensors with your LibreView account, so Abbott's app can take the
sensor back, and Sukrara can take it over again with one tap.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How fast do followers see my glucose?</summary>

About a second over the internet, at once over Bluetooth. If your phone stops sending, they see when the last reading
came and get a no-data alert.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which sensors does it read?</summary>

Directly: FreeStyle Libre 3 and 3 Plus. Directly, not yet tested on a real one: Dexcom G6, G7 and ONE+, Accu-Chek
SmartGuide, LinX / AiDEX X. Through Juggluco: FreeStyle Libre 2 and 2 Plus, Sibionics GS1, CareSens Air.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is there an iPhone version?</summary>

No. Followers can use an Android phone or [a Mac](/mac/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is it a medical device?</summary>

No, and no regulator has cleared or approved it. Check your sensor's app or a meter before you decide on a treatment.

</details>
