## Get it

<p><ConnectIqBadge /></p>

<Steps>

1. Install [Sukrara for Android](/android/) and Garmin Connect on your phone.
2. From the Connect IQ store, install a Sukrara watch face (<a href={STORES.connectIq('en', 'aura')}>Sukrara Aura</a>),
   the <a href={STORES.connectIq('en', 'app')}>Sukrara watch app</a>, the
   <a href={STORES.connectIq('en', 'field')}>Sukrara data field</a>, or all three.
3. Nothing to set up: every reading reaches the watch by itself.

</Steps>

## The Sukrara watch app

Your glucose in the glance list and on any watch face that takes complications, with its chart and your numbers a
press away.

<WatchScreens ids={['glance', 'app', 'numbers', 'complications']} />

**Log from your wrist.** Rapid or long-acting insulin, or carbs, saved in the phone's log.

<WatchScreens ids={['list', 'amount', 'hold', 'logged']} />

**Alerts and mute.** When Sukrara's alert rings on a locked phone, the watch shows it with its actions. With the phone
in your hand, only the phone alerts: no double alert, also once you lock it again.

<WatchScreens ids={['alertLow', 'alertVeryLow', 'muteLengths', 'muted']} />

**Never an old value as current.** The watch says what it's waiting for, and keeps what you log until the phone is back.

<WatchScreens ids={['old', 'warmup', 'queued', 'setup']} />

It looks the way your phone does; each watch can have its own settings in **Settings › Share with apps › Garmin**.

## The Sukrara data field

Your glucose on an activity's data screen, in any size of slot. It's saved with the activity, so Garmin Connect charts
it beside your heart rate and pace.

<WatchScreens ids={['fieldBand', 'fieldQuarterLow', 'fieldAlert', 'fieldMip']} />

Add it in the activity's settings › Data Screens, where it's *Sukrara Glucose* in the Connect IQ list, and turn its
full-screen alert on in › Alerts › Connect IQ.

## Requirements

- An Android phone with [Sukrara for Android](/android/) and Garmin Connect. Not an iPhone.
- **Sukrara Aura**: a round 390 × 390 AMOLED watch: Descent Mk3 / Mk3i 43 mm, Venu 3S, Venu 4 41 mm, vívoactive 6,
  Forerunner 570 42 mm, epix Pro (Gen 2) 42 mm, MARQ (Gen 2) and MARQ Aviator, Approach S70 42 mm.
- **The watch app and the data field**: any watch with Connect IQ 5 or newer: fēnix 7, 8, 9 and E, epix (Gen 2) and epix
  Pro, enduro 3 and 4, Forerunner 70, 165, 170, 255, 265, 570, 955, 965 and 970, Venu 2, 3 and 4, Venu Sq 2, Venu X1,
  vívoactive 5 and 6, Instinct 3 AMOLED and Instinct Crossover AMOLED, MARQ (Gen 2) and MARQ 3, Descent Mk3 and G2,
  Approach S50, S70 and S72, D2 Air X10, Mach 1 and Mach 2, with their Pro, Solar, S, X, Plus and Music editions.

## Questions

<details>
<summary>Does the watch measure my glucose?</summary>

No. It shows your sensor's reading, which Sukrara for Android gets from the sensor or from Juggluco.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it work with an iPhone?</summary>

No. It needs Sukrara for Android on the phone your watch is paired with.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it need the internet or an account?</summary>

No. Readings go from your phone through Garmin Connect, which your watch already uses. If one gets lost, the watch
asks the phone again within 5 minutes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What if my phone is out of reach?</summary>

The reading ages on the watch: late, then struck through, then *No reading since* with its time. What you log waits on
the watch. Back in reach, the current reading arrives within a minute.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do the watch's alerts replace the phone's?</summary>

No. The phone decides and rings every alert; the watch shows it too when it rings with the phone locked, until you
unlock the phone, swipe it away or mute it (then the watch stays quiet until its next reminder on a locked phone). Keep your phone's alerts on. On the Venu 2
family, Venu Sq 2 and D2 Air X10 the app shows no alerts of its own: the phone's notification, forwarded by Garmin
Connect, does.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I feel two buzzes for one alert</summary>

Garmin Connect also forwards the phone's notification. Turn Sukrara off in Garmin Connect's notification settings: the
app's alert stays.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I know my watch gets readings?</summary>

On the phone, **Settings › Share with apps › Garmin** shows each watch with the reading it has, or what to fix when it
can't get one.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can my favourite watch face or data field show Sukrara's glucose?</summary>

A face that takes complications can, with the Sukrara watch app installed. Faces and data fields made for xDrip+ can read
Sukrara's xDrip+ server: **Settings › Share with apps › Garmin › xDrip+ server**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which languages?</summary>

English and Spanish, as your watch is set.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I install them without the Connect IQ store?</summary>

Yes. Each app's [releases on GitHub](https://github.com/mauriciabad/sukrara/releases) (*Sukrara Aura*, *Sukrara watch
app*, *Sukrara data field*) have a zip with one
`.prg` file per watch model: connect the watch to a computer and copy yours into its `GARMIN/APPS` folder. It doesn't
update by itself; the store's copy replaces it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is it a medical device?</summary>

No, and no regulator has cleared or approved it. Check your sensor's app or a meter before you decide on a treatment.

</details>
