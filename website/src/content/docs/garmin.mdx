A watch face whose dial is the aura of your glucose: it gathers at your level and spreads the further out of range you
are. Each reading comes from [Sukrara for Android](/android/) within seconds.

Below, the face lives through a made-up day, 600 times faster: a low at night, meals, a high and its correction, a
sensor running out and a new one warming up, the phone left behind, a mute. Point at a part to see what it is, and pick
a dial. Or [try it yourself](/watch-face/): pick any state, change the reading, or speed up time.

## Get it

<p><ConnectIqBadge /></p>

<Steps>

1. Install [Sukrara for Android](/android/) and Garmin Connect on your phone.
2. Install Sukrara Aura from the Connect IQ store (coming soon) and make it your watch face.
3. That's it: Sukrara sends each reading to the watch by itself.

</Steps>

### On your phone

**Settings › Share with apps › Garmin** shows each watch and what it has, in words:

- *Descent Mk3i has the 10:09 reading*, or *waiting for a reading*.
- *Descent Mk3i isn't connected* when the watch is away, and *Sukrara Aura isn't on your watch* until you install it.
- *Needs Garmin Connect on this phone* or *Garmin Connect isn't answering · open it*, with the button that fixes it.
- *Updates every 5 minutes · Garmin Connect loses messages* when Garmin Connect drops them: the face asks the phone
  itself.
- **Send now** sends the current reading at once.

### In your language

English and Spanish, as your watch is: *Carbs* and *Hidratos*, *No reading since* and *Sin lecturas desde*.

## Requirements

- A Garmin watch with a round 390 × 390 AMOLED screen: Descent Mk3 / Mk3i 43 mm, Venu 3S, Venu 4 41 mm, vívoactive 6, Forerunner 570 42 mm, epix Pro (Gen 2) 42 mm, MARQ (Gen 2) and MARQ Aviator, Approach S70 42 mm. More watches will follow.
- An Android phone with [Sukrara for Android](/android/) and Garmin Connect, running in the background.

## Questions

<details>
<summary>Does it need the internet, an account or another app?</summary>

No. Sukrara on your phone sends each reading through Garmin Connect, which your watch already uses. If a message gets
lost, the face asks the phone again within 5 minutes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens when my phone is out of reach?</summary>

The reading ages by itself on the watch: late, then struck through, then *No reading since* with its time, and the
watch shows *No phone*. Back in reach, the current reading arrives within a minute.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it work with an iPhone?</summary>

No. It needs Sukrara for Android on the phone your watch is paired with.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I use another Garmin watch face or data field. Can it show Sukrara's glucose?</summary>

Faces and data fields made for xDrip+ can read Sukrara's xDrip+ server: **Settings › Share with apps › Garmin › xDrip+
server**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is it a medical device?</summary>

No, and no regulator has cleared or approved it. Check your sensor's app or a meter before you decide on a treatment.

</details>
