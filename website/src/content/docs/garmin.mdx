Sukrara puts your glucose on your watch three ways, each fed by [Sukrara for Android](/android/) within seconds. Use
one, two or all three:

- **[Watch faces](#watch-faces)**: Sukrara Aura, whose dial is the aura of your glucose.
- **[Apps](#apps)**: Sukrara, the watch app. Your glucose in the glance list and on any watch face that takes
  complications, insulin and carbs logged from the wrist, alerts you can answer there.
- **[Data fields](#data-fields)**: Sukrara data field, your glucose on the screen of a run, a ride or a hike, and in
  the activity once it's saved.

## Get it

<p><ConnectIqBadge /></p>

<Steps>

1. Install [Sukrara for Android](/android/) and Garmin Connect on your phone.
2. From the Connect IQ store (coming soon): pick a Sukrara watch face, add Sukrara to your glances, and put Sukrara
   data field on an activity's data screen.
3. That's it: Sukrara sends each reading to every one of them by itself.

</Steps>

## Apps

### Sukrara

The watch app, for the moments a Sukrara face isn't on: another face you like, a glance while you scroll, a meal you log without taking out
the phone.

#### At a glance

In the watch's glance list: the value and its arrow, your main ring (time in range, or what you chose on the phone),
and your last treatment or the second ring. Its time shows when the reading is late, Sukrara's pill when there is
something to do. Select it to open the app.

#### Two pages

- **The card**: the value big, your rings and last treatment beside it, a chart of the last hours with your ranges and
  treatment marks, and if you want the share of readings above, in and below range.
- **Your numbers**: time in ranges, average, GMI and variability, each with its goal met or not, over 1, 7, 14 or 30
  days.

Swipe or press UP / DOWN between them.

#### On any watch face

Sukrara's complications go on watch faces that take other apps' (the watch's own face editor on newer watches, Face It,
faces made for CGM+): your glucose, with its trend and the last 24 hours for faces that draw them, and **Log**, the
time since your last treatment, which opens logging when you tap it on a touch watch. An old reading shows as `---`, never as
current.

#### Log from your wrist

START, then rapid insulin, long-acting insulin or carbs, already in the order Sukrara would pick (carbs first when
you're low). Pick the amount with the buttons or one of your three usual amounts, then hold START until the ring
fills: letting go cancels. *Logged 4.5u rapid*, with **Undo** for 10 seconds, and the same entry is in the phone's log,
with the phone's quiet rules. The same kind logged in the last 15 minutes is said first.

Phone out of reach? The watch keeps the entry with its own time and sends it when the phone is back.

#### Mute, or remind less

From the same list, **Mute alerts** for 15 minutes to 2 hours or until you unmute: the phone's alerts and the watch's,
with **Undo**. While muted, the time left shows everywhere, with **Unmute** and **Change time**.

#### Alerts on your wrist

When Sukrara's alert rings on the phone, the watch shows it too: *Low 62 · Falling*, with the phone's three actions.
**Treatment** opens logging on carbs for a low, rapid insulin for a high; **Remind less** spaces the reminders, with
Undo; **Mute** silences that kind of alert for a while. A very low fills the screen in its colour.

Garmin Connect also forwards the phone's notification, so you may feel two buzzes for one alert. If you do, switch Sukrara off in
Garmin Connect's notification settings: the watch app's alert stays.

#### Its settings

It looks the way your phone does: rings, chart hours, treatment marks, report period. Each watch can have its own:
**Settings › Share with apps › Garmin**, then **Sukrara** under the watch, where every setting says *Same as the app*
until you change it. In the Connect IQ app, two of its own: the sparkline on alerts, and what the Log complication shows (time since, the amount, or both).

## Data fields

### Sukrara data field

Add it to an activity's data screen and your glucose stays in view while you move: the value and its recent line on a
whole or half screen, the value and arrow in a small slot. A low turns the slot its colour, an old reading is struck
through.

- **Its own alert**: a full-page alert during the activity when Sukrara's alert rings. Turn it on in the activity's
  settings › Alerts › Connect IQ.
- **Glucose in the activity**: every reading is saved with the activity, so Garmin Connect charts it beside your heart
  rate and pace. Its setting *Record glucose* turns it off.

## On your phone

**Settings › Share with apps › Garmin** shows each watch and what it has, in words:

- *Descent Mk3i has the 10:09 reading*, or *waiting for a reading*.
- *Descent Mk3i isn't connected* when the watch is away, and *No Sukrara app on your watch* until you install one.
- **Sukrara on your watch** lists the app and the data field, and **Watch faces** each Sukrara watch face, with when
  each last asked this phone (with two watches, each its own lists). **Sukrara** opens that watch's settings.
- *Needs Garmin Connect on this phone* or *Garmin Connect isn't answering · open it*, with the button that fixes it.
- *Updates every 5 minutes · Garmin Connect loses messages* when Garmin Connect drops them: each app asks the phone
  itself.
- *Messages stopped getting through · restart the watch* when none has arrived for 15 minutes; the apps keep asking
  the phone meanwhile.
- *The watch face isn't taking new readings · restart the watch* when the watch gets them but the face doesn't show
  them.
- *The watch face can't read this phone's messages · update it in the Connect IQ app* when the face is older than the
  phone.
- **Send now** sends the current reading at once.

- **Watch faces**: Sukrara Aura needs a round 390 × 390 AMOLED screen: Descent Mk3 / Mk3i 43 mm, Venu 3S, Venu 4
  41 mm, vívoactive 6, Forerunner 570 42 mm, epix Pro (Gen 2) 42 mm, MARQ (Gen 2) and MARQ Aviator, Approach S70
  42 mm. More watches will follow.
- **Sukrara and Sukrara data field**: every Garmin watch with Connect IQ 5 or newer: fēnix 7, 8, 9 and E, epix
  (Gen 2) and epix Pro, enduro 3 and 4, Forerunner 70, 165, 170, 255, 265, 570, 955, 965 and 970, Venu 2, 3 and 4,
  Venu Sq 2, Venu X1, vívoactive 5 and 6, Instinct 3 AMOLED and Instinct Crossover AMOLED, MARQ (Gen 2) and MARQ 3,
  Descent Mk3 and G2, Approach S50, S70 and S72, D2 Air X10, Mach 1 and Mach 2, with their Pro, Solar, S, X, Plus and
  Music editions. On the Venu 2 family, Venu Sq 2 and D2 Air X10 the watch app shows no alerts of its own: keep
  Sukrara on in Garmin Connect's notifications there.
- An Android phone with [Sukrara for Android](/android/) and Garmin Connect, running in the background.

## Questions

<details>
<summary>Does it need the internet, an account or another app?</summary>

No. Sukrara on your phone sends each reading through Garmin Connect, which your watch already uses. If a message gets
lost, each app asks the phone again within 5 minutes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens when my phone is out of reach?</summary>

The reading ages by itself on the watch: late, then struck through, then *No reading since* with its time, and the
watch shows *No phone*. What you log meanwhile waits on the watch. Back in reach, the current reading arrives within a
minute and your entries reach the phone.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it work with an iPhone?</summary>

No. It needs Sukrara for Android on the phone your watch is paired with.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do the alerts on my watch replace the phone's?</summary>

No. The phone decides and rings every alert; the watch shows the ones it rings while the phone is in reach. Keep your
phone's alerts on.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I use another Garmin watch face or data field. Can it show Sukrara's glucose?</summary>

A face that takes complications can show Sukrara's, with the watch app installed. A face made to read Sukrara asks
the phone for your glucose itself ([for developers](/developers/garmin-faces/)). Faces and data fields made for
xDrip+ can read Sukrara's xDrip+ server: **Settings › Share with apps › Garmin › xDrip+ server**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is it a medical device?</summary>

No, and no regulator has cleared or approved them. Check your sensor's app or a meter before you decide on a treatment.

</details>
