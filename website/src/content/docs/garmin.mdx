A watch face whose dial is the aura of your glucose: it gathers at your level and spreads the further out of range you
are. Each reading comes from [Sukrara for Android](/android/) within seconds.

[Try it in your browser](/watch-face/): pick a state, change the reading, or speed up time.

## Get it

Sukrara Aura is on its way to the Connect IQ store.

<Steps>

1. Install [Sukrara for Android](/android/) and Garmin Connect on your phone.
2. Install Sukrara Aura from the Connect IQ store and make it your watch face.
3. That's it: Sukrara sends each reading to the watch by itself.

</Steps>

## What it does

- The time, and your glucose in its band's colour with Sukrara's trend arrow, or the last hour as a small line.
- A level rail from 1:30 to 4:30: where you are between your limits, with the last 30 minutes as a fading trail.
- What to do now: *Carbs* or *Insulin* when needed; alerts held after a treatment or muted, with the time left.
- An old reading never looks current: late, then struck through in grey, then *No reading since* and its time.
- The dial reacts to your glucose: grain, contours, waterline, lasers or glow; or a calm map, or black.
- Fields of your choice: weather, steps, last treatment, time in range, sunset, a second time zone and more.
- Always-on and night looks that keep the time readable; a private mode shows the value only while you hold the
  screen.
- On the phone, **Settings › Share with apps › Garmin** shows which reading each watch has.
- In English and Spanish, as your watch is.

## Requirements

- A Garmin Descent Mk3i 43 mm, for now. More watches will follow.
- An Android phone with [Sukrara for Android](/android/) and Garmin Connect, running in the background.

## Questions

<details>
<summary>Does it need the internet, an account or another app?</summary>

No. Sukrara on your phone sends each reading through Garmin Connect, which your watch already uses. If a message gets
lost, the face asks the phone again within 5 minutes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens when my phone is out of reach?</summary>

The reading ages by itself on the watch: late, then struck through, then *No reading since* with its time, and the
watch shows *No phone*. Back in reach, the current reading arrives within a minute.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it work with an iPhone?</summary>

No. It needs Sukrara for Android on the phone your watch is paired with.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I use another Garmin watch face or data field. Can it show Sukrara's glucose?</summary>

Faces and data fields made for xDrip+ can read Sukrara's xDrip+ server: **Settings › Share with apps › Garmin › xDrip+
server**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is it a medical device?</summary>

No, and no regulator has cleared or approved it. Check your sensor's app or a meter before you decide on a treatment.

</details>
