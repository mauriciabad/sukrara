Four packs, eight alerts, three sounds each. Sukrara picks one of the three at random every time an alert goes off, so
it doesn't get repetitive. Every sound is made from code, with original melodies.

<SoundBoard />

The strip under each alert is its vibration on Android, drawn to scale (falls, thuds, clicks and rises; height is
strength). It plays with the sound, and alone when the phone is on vibrate, so you can tell a low from a high in your
pocket. Lows ring on the alarm volume.

Choose a pack in **Settings › Alerts › Sound pack**, or one sound for a single alert on its own page.
