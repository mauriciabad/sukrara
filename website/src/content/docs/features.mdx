The main things Sukrara does. Open one to learn more.

<Features>

<Feature id="everywhere" icon="widgets" title="Your glucose everywhere you look" summary="Status bar, home screen, lock screen and notification. On the Mac, the menu bar.">

- A Live Update chip in the status bar, or with root, next to the clock.
- Home screen widgets with the chart and your time in range.
- With root, a card on the lock screen: tap it to log without unlocking.
- The same card on every screen, with a trend arrow from your readings, minutes sooner than the sensor's.

[See it on Android](/android/)

</Feature>

<Feature id="alerts" icon="notifications_active" title="Discreet alerts, less alarm fatigue" summary="Each alert its own sound. Quiet once you've acted.">

- Sound packs with a different intensity for each alert.
- Quiet after insulin or carbs, and while glucose heads back into range.
- Treatment, Remind less and Mute right on the alert.
- Through Do Not Disturb, if you let them.

[Listen to the sounds](/sounds/)

</Feature>

<Feature id="sharing" icon="group" title="Shared live, without a cloud" summary="Family and friends follow your glucose on their phone or Mac.">

- Invite them with a link or a QR code.
- End-to-end encrypted over Nostr, or Bluetooth when they're nearby. No account, no server to host.
- They get alerts of their own, and are told about a very low nobody answers.

[How the sharing works](/open-glucose-stream/)

</Feature>

<Feature id="logging" icon="edit_note" title="Log in seconds" summary="Insulin, carbs and notes from a widget, the notification or an alert.">

- Set when it happened with one wheel, or 15 minutes ago with a tap.
- A NovoPen Echo® Plus logs its own doses.
- Followers you allow can log for you.

</Feature>

<Feature id="reports" icon="bar_chart" title="Reports your clinic knows" summary="Time in ranges, average, GMI and variability.">

- On the phone and the Mac, over 1 to 30 days.
- Your clinic keeps getting your data through LibreView or Tidepool.
- Import your history from LibreView or Tidepool, export it as a CSV.

</Feature>

<Feature id="sensors" icon="dia_sensor" title="Reads your sensor" summary="FreeStyle Libre 3 directly, other sensors through Juggluco.">

- Start a Libre 3 or 3 Plus with a tap of the phone.
- Libre 2, Sibionics GS1 and CareSens Air through Juggluco.
- Dexcom G6, G7 and ONE+, Accu-Chek SmartGuide and LinX directly, not yet tested on a real one.
- Reminders before your sensor ends.

</Feature>

<Feature id="mac" logo="apple" title="On your Mac too" summary="Your glucose or someone else's in the menu bar, with the same alarms.">

- Widgets on the desktop and in Notification Center.
- Log treatments and see the glucose report.
- Next to your phone, it gets your glucose over Bluetooth.

[Sukrara for Mac](/mac/)

</Feature>

<Feature id="reliable" icon="check_circle" title="Keeps working" summary="It restarts itself, and tells you when something is in the way.">

- Keeps monitoring after a restart, even before you unlock.
- Says when Android or macOS blocks something, with the fix.
- A test alert checks that alerts get through.

</Feature>

<Feature id="private" icon="privacy" title="Private, free and open source" summary="No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone.">

- Nothing leaves your phone until you turn something on.
- Free, with its source code public under the GPL-3.0.

[Privacy policy](/privacy/) · <a href={GITHUB}>Source code</a>

</Feature>
