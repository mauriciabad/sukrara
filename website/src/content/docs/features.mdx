## Alerts

<FeatureGrid>
  <FeatureTile icon="dia_very_low" title="Every kind of alert" shot="alert">Lows, highs, fast changes and no data, each at your thresholds.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="music_note" title="Discreet sound packs">A different sound and intensity for each alert.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="check_circle" title="Quiet once you've acted">After insulin or carbs, and while glucose heads back into range.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="bedtime" title="Night alerts">Their own thresholds and rules at night.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="snooze" title="Remind less or mute">Right on the alert. Mute them all for up to 8 hours.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="notifications_active" title="Through Do Not Disturb">If you let Sukrara through.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="notification_add" title="On your watch">Alerts as notifications of their own, if you want.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="group" title="Followers are told">When a very low gets no answer.</FeatureTile>
</FeatureGrid>

## Sharing and following

<FeatureGrid>
  <FeatureTile icon="share" title="Invite with a link or QR code" shot="share" shotWidth={300}>Single use. You approve each person.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="key" title="End-to-end encrypted">Over public Nostr relays. No account, no server to host.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="bluetooth" title="Bluetooth nearby">No internet needed when you're close.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="person" title="Follow someone">On Android or a Mac, with alerts of your own.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="water_drop" title="Yours or someone else's">One app wears or follows: pick it in setup, change it any time in Settings › Source. The Mac only asks where it comes from.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="edit_note" title="Followers can log">Insulin and carbs, if you allow them.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="person_remove" title="Told when someone stops">Your phone says who stopped following, even if their phone was offline then.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="history" title="Stop following, keep the history">Follow a Sukrara or read another source, never both: another source asks first, and leaving asks which settings and history stay.</FeatureTile>
  <FeatureTile icon="backup" title="New phone">Your followers come with you, from a recovery code or your password manager.</FeatureTile>
</FeatureGrid>
