## With Sukrara on your phone

Open the link on the phone with [Sukrara for Android](/android/) installed and choose **I follow someone**, or tap
**Open in Sukrara** above. Don't have the app yet? [Get it](/android/#get-it), then open the link again.

## With Sukrara for Mac

Click **Open in Sukrara** above: Sukrara shows who is inviting you, with **Follow** and **Cancel**. Or choose
**Follow Someone…** in Sukrara's menu bar panel and paste or drop the link. [Get Sukrara for Mac](/mac/#get-it).

## Is it safe?

The link holds no glucose and no key to it. It lets the app show who is inviting you (their name and picture,
end-to-end encrypted) and ask to follow; they approve each request. Nothing in it is sent to this website: the part
after the `#` never leaves your browser. An invite works once and for 24 hours.
