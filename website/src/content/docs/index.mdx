<Section title="Shared without a cloud" lead="No account to create and no server to run. An invite link or QR code, and you allow each person.">
  <Flow
    you="Your phone"
    youNote="reads the sensor"
    internet="Over the internet"
    internetNote="public Nostr relays, about a second"
    nearby="Nearby"
    nearbyNote="Bluetooth, no internet needed"
    phone="Their phone"
    mac="Their Mac"
  />
  - **End-to-end encrypted:** relays pass your readings on and can't read them. Your name and picture travel encrypted
    too, never public.
  - **You decide who follows:** remove someone and the keys change; everyone else carries on.
  - **Open:** the way Sukrara shares is becoming a standard any app can implement, the
    [Open Glucose Stream](/open-glucose-stream/).
</Section>

<Section title="Alerts that know what you just did">
