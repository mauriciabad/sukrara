One of [Sukrara's watch faces](/garmin/#watch-faces). The dial gathers at your level and spreads the further out of
range you are.

Below, the face lives through a made-up day, 600 times faster: a low at night, meals, a high and its correction, a
sensor running out and a new one warming up, the phone left behind, a mute. Point at a part to see what it is, and pick
a dial. Or [try it yourself](/watch-face/): pick any state, change the reading, or speed up time.

<FaceTour />

Every picture here is the face itself, drawn by its own code from made-up readings. The ones marked *moves* run on a
clock, so you see a state come and go.
