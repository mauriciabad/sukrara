Sukrara on your watch gets your glucose from Sukrara for Android on this phone, through Garmin Connect. With Sukrara
installed, this link opens its Garmin page instead of this one.

<Steps>

1. Get [Sukrara for Android](/android/#get-it) and set it up.
2. In Sukrara, open **Settings › Share with apps › Garmin** and check that **Send to Sukrara on your watch** is on.
3. Keep Garmin Connect on this phone, allowed to run in the background. The watch updates with the next reading.

</Steps>

More about the watch face, the watch app and the data field: [Sukrara for Garmin](/garmin/).
