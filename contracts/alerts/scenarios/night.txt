# Night alerts (#38, SPEC.md §1 "Night alerts"; format: SPEC.md §7): a second set of every kind's rule in force each
# day in a window of the wearer's local time (the scenarios run in UTC; `at HH:MM` is minute 0's time of day). Off by
# default: every scenario here turns it on (22:00–07:00 unless it says otherwise).

scenario At 22:00 a high under the night threshold clears; at 07:00 the day rules alert again
at 21:58
settings night=on night.high=250
  0  glucose 200  => High glucose          # 21:58, day: high from 180
  2               => clear high            # 22:00, night: high from 250
542               => High glucose          # 07:00, day again: a new episode, at once
543

scenario The switch wakes the timer between readings
at 21:58
settings night=on night.high=250
 0  readings every 5, glucose 200  => High glucose
 2                                 => clear high      # 22:00 on the timer, not at the next reading
 3

scenario A night low threshold of 80: 75 alerts only at night
at 21:55
settings night=on night.low=80
 0  glucose 75                         # day: in range
 5               => Low glucose        # 22:00: low below 80 at night
 6

scenario Very low always alerts at night, whatever the night set says
at 23:00
settings night=on night.very_low=off night.low=off
 0  glucose 50   => Very low glucose
 1

scenario The night repeat replaces the day's
at 22:30
settings night=on night.high.repeat=60
 0  glucose 200  => High glucose
60               => High glucose       # not at 15: the night repeat is 60 min
61

scenario A window that doesn't cross midnight
at 12:55
settings night=on night.from=13:00 night.to=14:00 night.high=off
 0  glucose 200  => High glucose
 5               => clear high         # 13:00: highs off in the window
65               => High glucose       # 14:00: the day rules again
66

scenario The followers' alerts follow the night rules too (the rules decide for both)
mirror
at 21:59
settings night=on night.high=off
 0  glucose 200  => High glucose; follower High glucose
 1               => clear high; follower clear high     # 22:00: highs off at night
 2
