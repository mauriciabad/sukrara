# What a phone's watches say (#248; SPEC §1 "On the wrist"): an alert whose notification is up on that phone and whose
# last ring found the phone locked, until the person unlocks it (seen). Each ring is judged by the lock at that moment.
# `wrist` compares it (`wrist <kinds>` / `wrist none` when it changes); every phone starts locked, `lock` / `unlock` /
# `swipe <kind>` (and `follower …`) change it. Format: SPEC §7.
