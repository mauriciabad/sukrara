# Online sources (docs/44 step 1, Nightscout): a reading every 5 min, each arriving minutes after it was taken

scenario An online source every 5 min, each reading 2 min late: a low alerts when the reading that shows it arrives
 0  readings every 5, glucose 100 age 2
 5  glucose 75 age 2
10  glucose 65 age 2   => Low glucose      # taken at 8, here at 10
15  glucose 64 age 2   => Low glucose      # its repeat, on the next reading
20  glucose 66 age 2   => Low glucose
25  glucose 85 age 2   => clear low

scenario A reading that arrives 16 min after it was taken never alerts; no data counts from its time
 0  source started
 1  glucose 50 age 16                       # taken at −15: too old to alert (§1: older than 15 min)
 2  sensor off
 5                     => No glucose data   # 20 min after it was taken, not after it arrived

scenario A low that arrives 14 min late alerts, and clears once it is older than 15 min
