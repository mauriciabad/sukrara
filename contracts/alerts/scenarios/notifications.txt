# What stays on screen between the alerts (#195; SPEC §1 "Showing an alert"). `shows` compares the silent shows
# (`show <headline>`) and their clears too; the sounds and their timing are the rules of the other files.

scenario Getting better shows the milder alert at once without sound; its sound waits for the repeat
shows
 0  glucose 56   => Low glucose
 1  glucose 55   => clear low; Very low glucose          # worse: at once, with sound
 2  glucose 56   => clear very_low; show Low glucose     # better: the low is on screen at once, silently
 3  glucose 55   => clear low; show Very low glucose     # very low again within its repeat: on screen, silently
 6               => Very low glucose                     # 5 min after the last alert: the repeat rings
 7

scenario A reading back in range for a moment takes the alert away; out again, it is back at once without sound
shows
 0  glucose 65   => Low glucose
 2  glucose 72   => clear low                            # in range: nothing low to show
 3  glucose 66   => show Low glucose                     # low again in the same episode: shown, no sound
 5               => Low glucose                          # the repeat rings as before
 6

scenario A high back over its threshold between repeats is shown again silently; the ring waits 15 min

 6  glucose 185  => show High glucose
15               => High glucose
16

scenario Once no data holds, the low and falling-fast alerts go: no data's own alert says the last value
settings no_data=10 falling_fast=on
 0  glucose 65 rate -2.5   => Low glucose; Falling fast
 1  sensor off
 5               => Low glucose                                    # the repeat (the reading is 5 min old)
10               => clear low; clear falling_fast; No glucose data
11  sensor on, glucose 64 rate -0.5   => Low glucose; clear no_data  # a new low episode: its first alert
12
