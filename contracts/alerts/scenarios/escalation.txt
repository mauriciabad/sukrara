# No answer to a very low (#39, SPEC.md §4 "No answer to a very low"; format: SPEC.md §7): when nobody answers a very
# low on the wearer's phone for 5 min, the followers that opted in get it as "Very low glucose · no response", once
# through their own mutes. An answer: `ack` (tap, swipe, opening the app), a notification action, Mute all, carbs.

scenario An unanswered very low escalates to the followers after 5 min
mirror
 0  glucose 50   => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose
 5               => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose · no response    # the repeat carries it
10               => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose · no response
11

scenario Opening the alert on the wearer's phone is an answer: no escalation
mirror
 0  glucose 50     => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose
 2  ack very_low
 5                 => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose
 6

scenario The wearer's Mute is an answer
mirror
 0  glucose 50       => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose
 1  mute very_low    => clear very_low
 5                   => follower Very low glucose
 6

scenario Answered after the escalation: the followers' alert is plain again, without sound
mirror
 0  glucose 50     => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose
 5                 => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose · no response
 7  ack very_low   => follower answered very_low
10                 => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose
11

scenario Mute all set before the very low is no answer: nobody heard it
mirror
 0  glucose 100, mute all 2h
 1  glucose 50     => follower Very low glucose
 6                 => follower Very low glucose · no response
 7

scenario The escalation breaks through the follower's own Mute once
mirror
 0  glucose 50                => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose
 1  follower mute very_low    => follower clear very_low
 5                            => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose · no response
10                            => Very low glucose        # the follower's own Mute holds for the repeats after it
11

scenario On the timer between readings: the newest very low is sent again, escalated
mirror
settings very_low.repeat=15
 0  readings every 3, glucose 50   => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose
 5                                 => follower Very low glucose · no response
 6

scenario Better before the wait is over: no escalation
mirror
 0  glucose 50   => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose
 3  glucose 60   => clear very_low; follower clear very_low
 5               => Low glucose; follower Low glucose          # the very low stretch is over
 6

scenario A follower that turned escalations off gets the very low as usual
mirror
settings follower.escalate=off
 0  glucose 50   => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose
 5               => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose
 6

scenario The wearer turned escalations off
mirror
settings escalate=off
 0  glucose 50   => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose
 5               => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose
 6

10               => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose · no response
11

scenario A restart on the wearer's phone keeps the escalation: no second one, the repeats still carry it

 5               => Very low glucose; follower Very low glucose · no response
 7  restart      => resume very_low
